# Approved voice evidence in CAM++ version 3

Startup first secures independent encrypted clips from retained recordings using the existing pinned recording, PCM hash, exact span and ownership checks. It then re-embeds each approved legacy clip into 192 dimensions. It never uses a name, a transcript centroid or somebody else's audio to manufacture evidence. Approval timestamps, ciphertext, provenance and action history stay intact. `voice_embedding_migrations` appends an attempt with person/sample IDs, source and vector hashes, result and reason; vectors and the derived cache commit together per clip. There is no partly committed vector. Successful clips are skipped on rerun; failed or interrupted attempts retry from ciphertext on the next launch. Legacy recording recovery can retry a previous failure within its original retention deadline; that deadline never renews.

An expired recovery grace period cannot make audio that is still normally retained unusable. That audio may retry without renewing the grace period or preventing ordinary retention/budget eviction. A converted legacy person stays not enrolled, including for provisional matching, until current evidence independently satisfies graduation; successful conversion of one remaining clip cannot create a half-profile.

Canonical input must be complete mono PCM s16le at 24 kHz, with matching declared and decoded duration; embedding resamples it to 16 kHz. Missing, corrupt, partial, wrong-rate, invalidated, unresolved or unembeddable evidence cannot become a recognition profile. Its approval/audit remains inspectable. Until the remaining current evidence independently meets enrollment requirements, the People badge uses the ordinary No voice profile presentation and the existing Enroll voice action. It never exposes a legacy or half-migrated vector to naming. Successfully migrated clips count toward the remaining work; three clean, consistent approved clips across two independently identified recordings are required. The review dialog shows current progress, clip durations and offered clips, with the existing playback/approval flow. Unusable samples can be removed through its existing sample controls.

Enrollment listings and action-time checks recompute spans, availability, overlap and echo eligibility. Review paging retains its structure but refreshes eligibility for unchanged notes too. Legacy evidence cannot consume the current sample cap or suppress a fresh review offer. Explicit rejection decisions remain durable. A new embedding-version component in candidate IDs separates fresh approval offers from historical ones. No labeling action enrolls a voice.

No owner-labeled CAM++ recording dataset is supplied in this repository/session that supports retuning recognition. Existing fork defaults remain uncalibrated: decision settings similarity floor 0.65, runner-up margin 0.03, minimum voiced duration 1.6 s and overlap tolerance 0.1 s; the existing uncalibrated recognition branches use floor 0.6 and live margin 0.08. These are inherited conservative guards together with approval, cross-recording consistency and roster vetoes, not measured CAM++ accuracy claims. Enrollment additionally uses own-window/cross-clip similarity 0.65, minimum RMS 0.01, clipping fraction 0.01 and echo correlation 0.88; eligible spans are 3–8 s. Upstream threshold tables do not decide names. Stored threshold settings without this embedding version reset to the fork's uncalibrated defaults. New calibration still requires the existing reviewed evidence, independent holdout and explicit settings save.

Synthetic tests validate migration mechanics and refusal behavior, not biometric quality. Real device clips and owner-labeled holdout recordings are needed for acoustic calibration and install-specific person outcomes.
